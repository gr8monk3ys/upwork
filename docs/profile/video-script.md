# Video introduction script (~60 seconds, ~155 words)

Read it twice before recording so it comes out conversational, not read.
Look at the lens, not the screen. Natural pace is about 150 words a minute,
so this lands right at a minute. Upwork caps intros at 2 minutes; shorter
wins.

---

Hi, I'm Lorenzo. I'm an AI engineer in Santa Monica.

I spent the last year at a startup in LA building a vision AI pipeline on
Google Cloud. It now serves over five thousand requests a day for ten
business clients. Before that I built data pipelines at a Department of
Energy lab, and I have a master's in data science from USC.

On Upwork I build RAG systems, chatbots, and full-stack web apps, with
Next.js on the front and FastAPI or Node behind it. My open source content
platform, Blog AI, runs in production with real users and real billing.

The way I work is simple. I write you a short plan first, get a working
deployment in front of you early, and keep you updated in plain English.

If you have docs you want to chat with, an MVP to ship, or a data pipeline
that keeps breaking, send me a message. Thanks.

---

## Recording notes

- Daylight or a lamp in front of you, not behind. Laptop camera at eye
  level (stack some books under it).
- Wear what you'd wear to a client call. Plain background beats a messy
  room; a bookshelf is fine.
- One take that sounds human beats five takes that sound rehearsed. Small
  stumbles are fine; restarting a sentence is fine.
- Upload from the profile page: Video introduction → the + button. Upwork
  wants a YouTube or Vimeo link, so upload it unlisted to YouTube first if
  the direct upload option doesn't appear.
